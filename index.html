About Our Center
What’s better than education? Accessible education! Our tutoring center aims to offer each student top notch tutoring services for affordable pricing. Our sliding scale system allows for just that. As students in high school, we noticed the same pattern over and over: students struggle in classes, and look for tutoring, only to find, well, paycheck punishing prices left and right. With our school not having a free tutoring system, we took matters into our own hands and started offering our own private tutoring services, and now, our own tutoring center. Most importantly, we love teaching! …and have been doing so for half a decade - as such, we’ve perfected our method. With us, classes are focused on you, the student, and your needs, from homework to test prep to general exploration. No two lessons are the same - we write each customized lesson for the student, informed by their abilities, questions, and concerns. The best part? You can reach out at any time! Whether you need help with conceptual understanding, course work, homework problems, or general advice, we will help as soon as we can.
